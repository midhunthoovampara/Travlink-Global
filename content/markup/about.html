Who We Are

Connecting Asia Through Travel, Trade & Shipping

It started the way most things like this do—with a favour. Someone needed a Qatar visa handled properly and had nowhere reliable to turn. Then someone else needed a supplier in China they could actually trust. Then a family needed a holiday planned without the usual runaround.

There was no company name yet. No office. No website. Just someone who kept getting asked, and kept saying yes.

Over five-plus years, that turned into a real practice—visas for Qatar, Kuwait, and the wider GCC; sourcing trips between Indian buyers and manufacturers in China and Vietnam; and travel planned for families, couples, and business teams. All of it grew through word of mouth, without a storefront.

Travlink Global is what happens when that quiet, referral-only practice finally gets a name, a platform, and a front door. We didn't start as an idea looking for a market—we're an experience that's simply stepping into the open.
