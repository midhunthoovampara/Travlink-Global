About Travlink Global

Connecting People, Places
& Opportunities.

Travlink Global is a Travel & Trade company built to make international opportunities more accessible, practical, and connected.

Our business operates through two core areas — Travel and Trade.

Travel

We help people explore the world through visa assistance, international travel experiences, curated group journeys, business travel, and carefully selected global experiences.

Trade

We help businesses connect with international markets through product sourcing, supplier coordination, shipping, customs clearance, and import-export support, with a strong focus on China and Vietnam.

What Connects It All

For us, Connect is more than a word. It represents the idea behind Travlink Global — connecting travellers with destinations, businesses with suppliers, and people with opportunities across borders.

We are continuously building a trusted international network of destination partners, suppliers, logistics providers, travel professionals, and businesses to make these connections easier.
