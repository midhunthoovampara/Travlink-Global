Who We Are

Connecting Asia
Through Travel, Trade &
Shipping

Travlink Global began with a simple observation:

People want to travel further. Businesses want to trade globally. And opportunities often exist on the other side of a border — but reaching the right people can be difficult.

That is where Travlink Global comes in.

We are building a platform that brings Travel, Trade and Connect together.

From a traveller searching for a new destination, to a business looking for a supplier in China, a company planning a factory visit, an entrepreneur exploring an international opportunity, or a client looking for a private travel experience — our role is to create the connection that helps move things forward.

We don’t see travel and trade as completely separate worlds:

A business trip can become
a sourcing opportunity.

A factory visit can become
a long-term partnership.

A journey can become
a new business connection.

An exhibition can become
the beginning of a global relationship.

And that is the story behind our name.
